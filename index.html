Ideas I work by

  • Determinism is evidence; non-determinism is judgement. Deterministic checks gather the evidence; model deliberation decides what it means.
  • Chaotic, constrained, coherent. Ungoverned agents are chaos: every unchecked change is a ticking time bomb. Bolting rules onto agents only trades chaos for babysitting. Coherent systems balance deterministic and non-deterministic checks at every stage, from discovery to deploy, and produce agents you can walk away from.
  • Beneficial friction. Fast agents need pools of deliberation where it matters.
  • Design systems that make hallucinating safe. Intelligence hallucinates, human and agent alike.
  • Safety is created in the ensemble. Nothing made by a solo worker is safe, human or agent.
