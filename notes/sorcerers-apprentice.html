The Sorcerer's Apprentice

November 24, 2026

What is happening in the story of the Sorcerer’s Apprentice? And can we improve our understanding of AI enabled-outcomes with it?

The apprentice in this scenario is designing for throughput. Brooms with buckets, marching and multiplying!

The apprentice is kicking off more sessions, more parallel agents, tests and reviewers are added to try to keep up as the amount of bugs shipped keeps increasing.

This is skilled work - it takes effort and engineering to create this amount of mayhem - and it's still orchestration. No lives can rely on what you’ve built - in fact, your work is fundamentally unsafe.

The sorcerer, on the other hand is designing for the best possible judgement on the best possible evidence.

Deterministic checks gather the evidence, juries deliberate, people decide where values or product are at stake, and the rules live in gates rather than prompts, so they don't drift.

This comes down to what we expect out of these AI models.

Alignment is something you hope for inside the model; governance is something you can check in the system.

Sorcerers - mature agentic system designers - govern the work of the agents. That’s what keeps it safe, sane, and something we actually want.
