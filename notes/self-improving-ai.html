Self-Improving AI Requires Good Leadership Skills

November 23, 2026

We can't say this too many times - your AI harness/platform/approach is going to accurately reflect your mental models.

That means, if you don't know how to delegate in your work life, you will find it challenging to truly delegate to AI agents.

Which means, you have slapped a glass ceiling on your system. It will never grow past that point where it needs babysitting.

So you need to put on your leadership hat. Remove obstacles from your agents - give them the support they need. Clarify goals for them. Make tasks winnable. Get out of their way. Remove silos between working agents, give them the support to talk to each other.

Make all of this happen around a core learning-focused working Heartbeat.

There will come a tipping point where the system has enough support, enough clarity, that suddenly - you see it improving on its own. Agents will begin sharing feedback, bugs, and friction between ensembles, and folding those fixes into the work where it needs to be done. Context sharing will widen out in emergent and surprising ways.

It was never your job to build a non-deterministic system. These kinds of systems expand through an almost organic process of growth. This isn't engineering exactly; it's more like tending a garden.
