AI Needs a Heart

November 13, 2026

All agentic work, to be safe, must take place in a coherent loop of some kind.

Every mature industry has identified its own, phased loop, and often there are several to choose from, competing with each other.

At Clickchain, we call this loop a “Heartbeat”. The smallest unit of coherent work.

In the military, this Heartbeat might be called the OODA loop - Observe, Orient, Decide, Act.

In complex system interventions, it might be called the Adaptive Action - What, So What, Now What.

In QA/continuous improvement, it might be called PDSA - Plan, Do, Study, Act.

You may also have heard of Double-Loop Learning - Assumptions, Actions, Results.

However you describe your Heartbeat, the most important element of a Heartbeat is that it contains learning. Otherwise it’s just a cog in a machine - and agentic systems, regardless of appearance and the hardware involved, are not deterministic. They are non-deterministic, and so need a non-deterministic heart.
